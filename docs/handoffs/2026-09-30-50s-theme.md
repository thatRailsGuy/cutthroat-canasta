# 1950s Card Night theme: running notes

Branch `feat-50s-theme`. Design: the "C · 1950s Card Night" and "Big moments (animated)" boards on the design canvas. Desktop first; mobile layout is out of scope.

## Status

Done for desktop, and checked in headless Chrome at 1440 px. To see it: `npm run dev:server` and `npm run dev:web`, then open `http://localhost:5173/dev/table`. That page shows a made-up three-player game, and its **Preview controls** (bottom left) act out each animation.

## What changed

- **Look.** A gingham tablecloth; cream cards with thick ink outlines and a hard offset shadow; Shrikhand for display, Karla for body text, Caveat for handwriting. The tokens are in `global.css`. The home, lobby and rules pages get the same look. The rules page sits on a paper panel, so long text stays readable.
- **Cards.** Corner index with a large centre pip. Teal backs with a starburst (`CardBack`). Small cards show one corner, "K♥", for meld columns.
- **Melds.** Overlapping card columns, as on the Casino Felt board, with a "3 of 7" caption. A finished canasta stays squared up with one card on top (the playtest decision), and gets a tilted **Natural** (red) or **Mixed** (teal) ribbon.
- **Table layout.** Logo and opponent panels at the top, then the stock and pile, then your panel. A right-hand column holds a handwritten **score pad** (one line per round, the leader underlined in red) and the **Table talk** feed.
- **Opponent panels.** A coloured initial avatar, a small fan of card backs for the hand count, and compact meld columns.
- **Pile.** Cards peek out under the top card. When the pile is frozen for you it gets an ice ring, a snowflake badge and a speech bubble saying why. A black 3 on top gets a red octagonal **Stop** sign.
- **Animations** (`TableEffects.tsx`, driven by new feed events):
  - Drawing: a back leaves the stock, flips in the air, and lands on the drawn card in your hand. For an opponent it lands on their fan of backs.
  - Picking up the pile: your new cards fly from the pile to their slots in your hand, one after another. For an opponent, blank cards fly to their fan.
  - Discarding: your card flies from its place in your hand to the pile. An opponent's card leaves their fan face down and turns over on the way. A discarded wild lands sideways, as the pile shows it.
  - A canasta: a gleam passes over it and a rubber stamp comes down ("Natural! +500").
  - A red 3: a mustard "+100" pill pops up over the player's red 3s.
  - Your turn: gold rings ripple out from the avatar.
  - Round end: new totals roll up like an adding machine.
  - With reduced motion, none of them play.

## Decisions

- **Branched from `main`**, which already includes the `feat-playtest-todo` work (canasta piles, score sheet, drawn-card highlight).
- **Fonts are committed to the repo** (`apps/web/src/fonts/`, the Latin subsets only, about 160 KB, with `OFL.txt`), not loaded from Google Fonts. This means no third-party request, and it works on a LAN with no internet. I did not use the `@fontsource` npm packages, because `npm install` rewrote the lockfile at the time. That was a bug in npm 11.6.0, fixed by upgrading npm.
- **Kept the squared-up canasta** from the playtest list, rather than the fanned column in the mockup. Only unfinished melds are columns.
- **A straight hand, not a fan.** Hands can grow past 20 cards after a pickup, and a fan gets hard to click. Selected cards rise and get a mustard underline.
- **The Scores button moved onto the score pad.** "Quit game" stays in your panel.
- **Animations use the Web Animations API on a layer outside React.** They copy the real card elements, so a flying card looks exactly like the one it lands on. More than three new events at once (a reconnect or catch-up) animate nothing.
- **Added a dev-only page at `/dev/table`** (`src/dev/PreviewPage.tsx`). It is lazy-loaded behind `import.meta.env.DEV`, so it is left out of builds. Delete it if you don't want it in the repo.
- **Stock button text** is now "Draw a card (N left)" instead of "Draw (N left)".

## Questions for you

1. **Mobile.** Under 1000 px the right-hand column drops below the table, but nothing else is tuned for phones. That is the next piece of work.
2. **Opponent panels with many melds** get tall, which pushes the pile down. This is fine for 3–4 players with a few melds each. Should melds for 5+ players collapse?
3. **The rule examples on the rules page** use the new cards. I didn't restyle the example boxes.
